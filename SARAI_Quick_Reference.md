# SARAI Quick Reference Guide
**For Front Desk Staff**

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

### New Booking (5 Steps)
1. Click **"New Booking"** → Fill guest details (Name, Contact, Type: Org/Non-Org)
2. Select dates → System shows available rooms
3. Click rooms to select (or use **"Mix & Match"** for smart allocation)
4. Enter payment details (advance auto-calculated)
5. Click **"Create Booking"** → Booking slip downloads

**Same-Day Booking**: Advance = ₹0 automatically

---

## ✅ Check-In (4 Steps)
1. Find booking → Click **"Check In"**
2. Fill guest info (Age, Gender, Address, Members)
3. Collect balance payment (if due)
4. **Confirm** → For Org guests, print and give Org Data Form

**Org Data Form**: Guest fills manually, keep paper copy (not digital)

---

## ✅ Check-Out (3 Steps)
1. Find booking → Click **"Check Out"**
2. Review charges (Room + License + Extra beds)
3. Collect balance → **Confirm** → Print bill, get signatures

---

## 🔄 Amend Booking
1. Find confirmed booking → Click **"Amend"**
2. Change dates/rooms → System shows cost difference
   - **Green** = Refund due (no payment needed)
   - **Red** = Additional payment required
3. Fill payment if needed → **Confirm**

---

## 🎨 Mix & Match Rooms

**When to Use**: Guest needs multiple rooms but single room not available for all nights

**How**:
1. During booking, click **"Mix & Match Rooms"**
2. View optimal combination (color-coded rooms per night)
3. **Accept** recommendation OR **Customize** manually
4. Complete booking normally

**PDF**: Shows room schedule for each night

---

## 💡 Quick Tips

### Guest Types
- **Org**: Organization personnel → Org rates + Color category + Org Data Form
- **Non-Org**: General public → Non-Org rates (higher)

### Rates (Per Night Per Room)
| Type | Cat I | Cat II |
|------|-------|--------|
| Org | ₹500 | ₹400 |
| Non-Org | ₹600 | ₹470 |

### Payment Modes
- **Cash**: No extra details
- **UPI**: UPI ID or Phone
- **Bank**: Bank Name, Account, IFSC

### Validation Rules
- Mobile: 10 digits, starts with 6-9
- Aadhaar: 12 digits
- IFSC: Format ABCD0123456

## 📄 PDFs Generated

1. **Booking Slip**: At booking creation (anytime reprint)
2. **Org Data Form**: At check-in for Org guests only
3. **Bill (Checkout Receipt)**: At check-out (anytime reprint)

## ⚠️ Common Issues

**No rooms showing**: Try different dates or use Mix & Match

**PDF not downloading**: Check popup blocker, allow downloads

**Amendment error**: Remove payment details if refund scenario (green)

**Check-in blocked**: Ensure booking is "Confirmed" status
